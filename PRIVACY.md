# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Pinterest Analytics application ("the App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information from Pinterest API
When you authorize the App to access your Pinterest account, we may collect:
- **Account Information**: Username, account type, profile image URL
- **Board Information**: Board names, descriptions, pin counts, follower counts
- **Pin Information**: Pin titles, descriptions, links, creation dates
- **Analytics Data**: Impressions, engagements, clicks, saves (if available for your account type)

### 1.2 Technical Information
- OAuth tokens required for API authentication
- Local configuration settings

### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Passwords or login credentials
- Personal messages
- Financial information
- Third-party tracking data

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- **Provide Analytics**: Generate reports and insights about your Pinterest account performance
- **Display Statistics**: Show board and pin statistics in the application interface
- **Export Reports**: Create downloadable reports in various formats (JSON, CSV, Excel)

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local Storage
All data retrieved from Pinterest is stored **locally on your device only**. The App does not transmit your data to any external servers.

### 3.2 Token Security
- OAuth access tokens are stored in local `.env` files
- Tokens are never shared with third parties
- You can revoke access at any time through Pinterest settings

### 3.3 Data Retention
- Data is retained only as long as needed for analytics purposes
- Generated reports are stored locally until you delete them
- You can delete all stored data by removing the application files

---

## 4. Data Sharing

### 4.1 We Do NOT Share Your Data
Your data is **never shared, sold, or transferred** to any third parties. This includes:
- Advertising networks
- Data brokers
- Analytics services
- Other companies or individuals

### 4.2 Third-Party Services
The App communicates only with:
- **Pinterest API** (api.pinterest.com) — to retrieve your account data

---

## 5. Your Rights

### 5.1 Under GDPR (European Users)
You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Objection**: Object to processing of your data

### 5.2 Under CCPA (California Users)
You have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether your data is sold or disclosed
- Say no to the sale of personal information
- Access your personal information
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

### 5.3 Exercising Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights:
1. Revoke app access in Pinterest settings: https://www.pinterest.com/settings/security
2. Delete local application files and data
3. Contact us via GitHub Issues

## 7. International Data Transfers
